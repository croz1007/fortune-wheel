HOW TO PLAY

Friday Wheel Instructions

Classroom-safe text filtering is always enabled for topics, puzzles, player names, and solve attempts.

  1. Click Create New Game.
  2. Enter the topic/category and the puzzle answer.
  3. Add every player who will participate, then click Start Game.
  4. The active player spins the wheel and then chooses a consonant.
  5. Each matching consonant earns the wheel value once for every occurrence in the puzzle.
  6. A vowel costs 250 points total, no matter how many times it appears.
  7. An incorrect consonant or vowel ends the turn.
  8. BANKRUPT resets the active player's current-game score to zero and ends the turn.
  9. LOSE A TURN immediately moves play to the next player.
  10. A player may attempt to solve during their turn. Punctuation and spacing do not have to be typed exactly.
  11. When a new game is created, current-game scores are added to each returning player's overall total.
  12. Use Theme to change the surrounding interface. The wheel itself always keeps its classic appearance.
